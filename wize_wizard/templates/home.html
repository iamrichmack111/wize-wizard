WHAT DO I DO FIRST?

The guided Wize path

1 · Choose a projectName the thing you are trying to figure out.
2 · Answer one strategic questionUse: I need to ___ so that I can ___.
3 · Ask WHY three timesEvery Why is another complete Wize statement: Wish → Dream → Fantasy.
4 · PERT-test the NeedThe Need is the Goal. Estimate Best / Most Likely / Worst and stress.
5 · Plan executionReview tasks, management structure, supervisors and communication channels.
6 · Use strategy labs when neededMarket, finance, risk and operations lessons explain what each tool means before you use it.
7 · Review the Final Project PlanSee scope, goals, Why logic, PERT, tasks, team design, assumptions and handbook tips in one report.

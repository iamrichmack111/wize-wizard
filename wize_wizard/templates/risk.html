WHAT WIZE IS DOING

Think of it as 5,000 practice futures.

Wize creates thousands of reasonable versions of the outcome around your estimate. It then shows three planning levels:

TypicalAbout half of simulated outcomes finish by here.
SaferA stronger planning buffer for important work.
Very cautiousA high-protection plan when failure is costly.

The technical names are P50, P80 and P95. You can learn those later; you do not need them to use the tool.
